Quick rules

  • Everyone may cross the sum of the two white dice in any one row.
  • The active player may also cross white + one coloured die in that colour's row.
  • Cross left to right. Skipped numbers can't be filled later.
  • You need 5 crosses in a row before crossing its last number. That locks the row, and the lock counts as a cross.
  • If the active player crosses nothing, they take a −5 penalty.
  • The game ends when two rows are locked or someone takes a 4th penalty.
